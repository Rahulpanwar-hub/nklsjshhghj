📖 3-Step Rainbow Physics

NCERT Class 10

Step 1: Refraction & Dispersion (Point A)

White sunlight enters the water droplet (n ≈ 1.33), slows down, bends toward the normal, and splits into 7 colours (VIBGYOR).

Step 2: Internal Reflection (Point B)

The dispersed rays hit the back inner surface of the raindrop and reflect internally back toward the front of the drop.

Step 3: Refraction Outward (Point C)

Rays refract again as they exit into air, widening the 7-colour fan at 40.6° (Violet) to 42.3° (Red) toward the observer.
